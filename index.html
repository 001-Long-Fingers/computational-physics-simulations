Numerical Methods

Planned reusable solvers, studied for stability and error.

yₙ₊₁ = yₙ + h·f(tₙ, yₙ)
Euler Method

First-order baseline integrator — simple, but error-prone over time.

xₙ₊₁ = 2xₙ − xₙ₋₁ + a·h²
Verlet Integration

Symplectic, energy-conserving — well suited to orbital and chaotic systems.

k₁,k₂,k₃,k₄ → weighted avg
Runge-Kutta (RK4)

Fourth-order accuracy for smooth, high-precision trajectories.
